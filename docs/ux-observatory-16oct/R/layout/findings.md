# Observatory round 3 · layout robustness and the phone (lens R/layout)

Reviewed: `A/mock-A.rendered.html` and `C/mock-C.rendered.html`, rendered by me at 390 (true phone layout via `A/shoot_phone.sh`), 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 wide (`A-*.png`, `C-*.png` in this folder). Numbers below are measured in the DOM (a small harness in `tmp/measure.sh`: element boxes, `scrollWidth`, where the first section starts), not eyeballed. The fixes are shown working in `demo-layout.html` (`demo-390/768/1024/1440.png`), built from `__DATASET__` with the real sample data.

Widths that matter: **390** (Brett's phone), **~1000** (the Observatory inside the importer's panel page), **1440** (his monitor). 768 and 1920 were checked too.

## The five things that matter most

1. **C breaks at the panel's width.** At 768 and 1024 the page gets a horizontal scrollbar (document `scrollWidth` 1107 px at both widths: the "Latest capture on each scope" table has fixed percentage columns and `nowrap` cells, and it is kept in a two-column row down to 700 px). The Projects lanes grid needs 1028 px (150+132+220+350+128 plus gaps) so at 1024 and 768 the **Next** column is clipped off by the card's `overflow:hidden`: the one thing each lane is there to say. See `C-1024.png`, `C-768.png`, `tmp/C-1024-projects.png`. **Severity: blocker** (that is the width the page is actually used at).
2. **Both headers eat the first phone screen.** Before the first section starts: A **431 px**, C **487 px** (C: title, three-line freshness, the Last-night card, the View selector, then a 150 px legend strip). On an iPhone (~660 px visible) that is the whole first screen. The thing Brett opens the page for from the sofa, "what should I process", is 2.5 screens down in A and about 4 screens down in C. **Fix:** a header that is at most ~190 px on a phone (title, one hero line, share bar, a two-column key, one freshness line), a sticky 52 px bar with the View selector, then **Last night (one line per session) and Process next (top 3)** before anything else. The demo's first section starts at **272 px**, and the first queue card is on the first screen. **Severity: high.**
3. **The nav breaks between 700 and ~1000 px, in both.** A puts the tabs in a sideways scroller with the scrollbar hidden: at 768 "Equipment" is cut mid-word and "Data health" is off-screen with nothing to say "swipe" (`A-768.png`). C has `overflow:visible` tabs inside a header with `overflow:hidden`, so at 768 "Data health" is simply clipped and unreachable (`C-768.png`, right edge 847 px on a 768 viewport). **Fix:** one sticky bar: tabs on one row from 900 up, tightened (8 px padding, 13 px) and allowed to wrap between 700 and 899, a labelled **View** `<select>` under 700. Never a hidden scroller. **Severity: high.**
4. **Tap targets.** Measured at 390: A's scope-name buttons **20 px** tall, "Review names" / "See the rows" **24 px**, the View select **31 px**, "All datasets in Pipeline" links 27 px, filter chips ~26 px. C: status pills 23 px, the 90-nights / a-year / all-time buttons **25 px**, "show all 23" 30 px, the attention buttons 32 px, View select 36 px. Nothing in either reaches 44 px. **Fix:** on a phone every primary control is 44 px tall (select, Next pill, "show all", back link); whole rows/cards are the tap target so the pill does not have to carry it alone; chips 36 px with 8 px gaps. The demo measures clean at 390 (only the Next pill at 44 and the row itself). **Severity: high.**
5. **Timelines are not drawn for the width they land in.** A draws the project-card timeline into a fixed 330-unit viewBox and lets the browser scale it, so text scales with it and a year of Andromeda (22 FRA400 sessions) becomes one red smear with month labels sitting on top of dots (`A-1024-lower`, `A-desk.png`); at 900–1100 the six-scope strip has ~13 px per night against 28 px dots, so the 107PHQ pair on 2 Oct overlaps. On the phone A stacks the six scopes to six blocks of ~95 px (**570 px** for 30 nights). C's lane axis labels collide ("Nov2026Mar May Jul Sep") and C's six scopes never share one picture at all. **Fix (in the demo):** draw every timeline at its real pixel width and redraw on resize (text never scales); cluster sessions whose dots would touch into a **capsule with a count** ("9", "13"); month labels spaced by the width of the previous label; the six-scope strip is **one SVG with six lanes sharing one axis** at every width, with the names/text in columns on desktop, a sub-line on tablet, and on the phone the names inside the SVG (22 px lanes, 150 px for all six) followed by a short text list (active scopes, then one "Quiet for 30 nights: S50 …" line). **Severity: high.**

## The View selector on phones

Keep a native `<select>` (it opens the OS picker, works with VoiceOver and a keyboard, costs no code) but: 44 px tall, full width of the bar, labelled "View", with the counts in the option text ("Data health · 2 to check"), a drawn chevron so it reads as a control, and **sticky** in the same bar as the ← Importer link. A's is 31 px with a grey label on a dark header; C's is better (36 px, full width) but scrolls away.

## The six-lane timeline at 390: what it should become

One SVG, six lanes of 22 px in contract order, scope short-names inside the SVG on the left (62 px), empty nights as 2 px dots, sessions as dots capped at r 8, the quiet band, and three axis labels at most ("17 Sep", "last night"). Below it, the words: one line per scope that was out ("FRA400 7.1 h over 4 nights · latest: Horsehead Nebula, 16 Oct · 1.8 h") and one line for the quiet scopes. ~330 px in all against A's 570. The picture stays comparable across scopes because they share one axis.

## One scrollbar: how the page should sit inside the panel

- The panel should **open the Observatory as a page of its own** (a route on the same local server), not render it inside the panel's page or an iframe. One document, one scrollbar, `position:sticky` works, the phone's address bar collapses normally.
- The way back is the **← Importer** link at the left of the sticky bar (arrow only under 900). The bar is the only chrome: tabs, the Data-health count, the "worked out" stamp on wide screens.
- If an iframe must stay for a release: `scrolling="no"`, `overflow:hidden` on the inner `html`, and the inner page posts its `scrollHeight` to the parent on load and resize so the frame grows to fit; the outer page is then the only scroller. Sticky bars still will not stick in that arrangement, which is why the route is the real fix.
- No inner `overflow:auto` anywhere in the page (A's tab strip is the one offender today).
