# Observatory round 3 · lens: motion, hover, focus, loading, live refresh

Reviewer: interaction design. Mocks reviewed: A (`A/mock-A.html`), C (`C/mock-C.html`), with B and the live page (`observatory-template.html`) as reference. The demo of every fix is `demo-motion.rendered.html` in this folder (open it in a browser; the yellow bar at the top is demo controls only). Frame strips: `strip-load-in.png`, `strip-expand.png`, `strip-range-and-done.png`. Single shots: `demo-desk.png`, `demo-hover.png`, `demo-keyboard.png`, `demo-rebuilding.png`, `demo-updated.png`, `demo-reduced-motion.png`, `demo-projects-tab.png`, `demo-phone.png`, `demo-phone-tap.png`, `demo-phone-tap2.png`. Every number and easing named below is in `motion-spec.md`.

The short version: **A and C are good pictures, and both are completely still.** Between them there is not one CSS transition, not one animation, no `prefers-reduced-motion` rule, no keyboard path to a dot, no touch path to a tooltip, and no idea of "the record just changed". Every state change in both is a hard cut or a full re-render. The live page is actually ahead of both mocks here (it has a tooltip that follows focus, a dot grow-in, a sliding drawer and a reduced-motion rule), and the blend must not lose that.

## The five things that matter most

1. **Nothing moves, and what changes re-paints (A and C, everywhere).** C's range buttons rebuild the lane HTML from scratch (`box.innerHTML = …`) and do it again on every window resize; A's tab switch toggles `display` and jumps to the top; C's "show all 23" and "last 3 sessions per scope" swap whole tables. A page that re-paints feels like a spreadsheet recalculating; Brett asked for alive. **Fix:** one set of motion tokens (`--dur-*`, `--ease-*`) and four choreographies: load-in, things moving to new places, things expanding, and the live refresh. Demo: all of it. *Severity: high.*
2. **The project card cannot be opened, or opens with a bang (A: "Projects that moved lately"; C: "Projects").** A's cards are `div`s with a click that sends you to another tab; they are not focusable at all. C uses `<details>` with the marker hidden: no chevron, no hover, no `aria-expanded`, and when Andromeda opens its four lanes push the whole page down in one frame with nothing scrolling to keep it in view. This is the one picture Brett said he liked (lanes with the quiet band), so how it arrives matters most. **Fix:** a card header that is a real button (chevron, `aria-expanded`, Enter/Space, Escape to close, focus returned on Close), and the lanes open as a **full-width panel under the card's row** so the other cards never jump: height eases over 480 ms, the panel's contents fade in 60 ms later, the dots sweep in, and the page scrolls only as far as needed to show the panel. Demo: click any card; `strip-expand.png`. *Severity: high.*
3. **Tooltips: A flickers, C can't be read (A: every `data-tip`; C: every `<title>`).** A has one global `mousemove` handler with zero delay that follows the cursor; the job cards and project cards carry their whole evidence text as a tooltip, so sweeping the pointer across the page is a constant flicker, and on a phone (no mousemove) they never appear. Keyboard users get nothing. C uses native `<title>`/`title=`: a one-second delay, unstyled, different in Safari, Edge and Chrome, invisible to keyboard and touch, and impossible to put a coloured key in. **Fix:** one tooltip element anchored to the **mark**, not the cursor (it stops jittering and can carry a caret); opens after 90 ms, re-opens instantly while "warm" (within 400 ms of the last close) so walking along a strip feels continuous; fades 200 ms; value first, label second, a short stroke of the scope colour as the key; the same tooltip on keyboard focus; a tap pins it on touch and a second tap acts; a 24 px invisible hit circle behind every dot. Only marks get tooltips: cards and jobs say what they are in text. Demo: hover or Tab to a strip; `demo-hover.png`, `demo-keyboard.png`, `demo-phone-tap.png`. *Severity: high.*
4. **Changing the range redraws the dots instead of moving them (C project lanes; the live page's scope card; A has no range at all).** The live page re-runs its grow-in animation on every range click, so the dots "re-spawn"; C rebuilds them. When the dots **slide** to their new places Brett's eye can follow one session as the scale changes; a redraw forces him to re-read the whole strip. C also draws the lanes with `preserveAspectRatio="none"` in a 600-unit box squeezed into a 150 px cell, so every circle is an ellipse (and the in-bar labels of its integration bar are stretched the same way). **Fix:** dots keyed by `scope|night`; a range change sets a new `transform` on each (320 ms, `--ease-move`), the quiet band slides and scales, dots leaving fade and shrink, dots arriving pop; the SVG is sized to its real width so a circle stays a circle at every size, including the phone. Demo: 30 nights → a year; `strip-range-and-done.png`. *Severity: high.*
5. **Neither mock knows the record can change under it (A header "Built…", C "worked out…").** Both print a static timestamp. Today the panel's Refresh button reloads the whole Observatory frame (white flash, scroll and tab lost) and says "Working it out…" in the panel, outside the page. Brett's importer rebuilds this dataset on every ship and every nightly job, so "is this current?" is a question the page must answer by itself. **Fix:** a freshness pill in the header with five states (live · N min ago / a day old / rebuilding… / updated just now / importer not answering), a 30-second poll of `/api/status`, and when the record changes the page **redraws in place**: the hero counts from the old value to the new (the only count-up on the page, because the number really changed), changed numbers get a wash that fades, the new session pops into its strip, the last-night card crossfades, and a toast says what arrived with a Show button. Demo: "Simulate a rebuild"; `demo-rebuilding.png`, `demo-updated.png`. Needs two small additions upstream (see the spec). *Severity: high.*

## The rest, ranked

6. **Keyboard (A and C).** A: `.proj`, `.job` and the last-night rows are clickable `div`s with no `tabindex` or role; SVG dots are not focusable; A has a `:focus-visible` rule and nothing to focus. C: no `:focus-visible` rule at all (the browser default ring is faint on the night-sky header), tabs are `<a href="#">` that jump to the top, and the only keyboard-reachable thing is the `<summary>`. **Fix:** tabs as a roving-tabindex `tablist` (← → Home End); each strip is **one** tab stop (not 30 dots) and ← → walk its nights with the tooltip following, Enter opens the project; range buttons are a radio group (← →); cards are buttons; Escape closes tooltip and panel; focus returns to the card header on Close. A key legend sits under the strips. Demo: `demo-keyboard.png`, `demo-projects-tab.png`. *Medium-high.*
7. **No load-in (A and C).** Both appear in one frame. The live page has a nice dot grow-in but fires it on every redraw. **Fix:** a one-time choreography: the header's two halves rise (0 and 120 ms), the share bar's segments grow left to right (260 ms + 60 ms each), the body blocks rise with a 70 ms stagger from 150 ms, each ring draws its segments in turn, and each strip's dots sweep in left to right (200 ms + up to 420 ms) **after** their block has arrived. Total 1.2 s, nothing below the fold is delayed by more than the sixth block. **Decision: the hero number does not count up on load.** 292.8 is a fact, not a progress bar; a number that spins on arrival looks like it is still being worked out, and tabular digits flickering is the opposite of professional. It rises with its block and the share bar underneath does the "alive" work (it is data: the shares really are proportions). The count-up is reserved for a refresh, where the value genuinely changed. Demo: Replay load-in; `strip-load-in.png`. *Medium.*
8. **Hover states (A: colour-only, instant; C: none).** A's `.proj:hover` and `.trow:hover` change the border colour with no transition, so they snap; C has not a single `:hover` rule, and neither has `:active`. **Fix:** cards lift 1 px with a soft shadow over 120 ms; buttons lift and press (`scale(.97)` for 60 ms); dots scale 1.25 and take an ink stroke; chips take a wash; the sliding underline follows the active tab. *Medium.*
9. **Jobs never go away (C "Needs your attention"; A "To check").** The lists are static; "Nothing to do" is a button that does nothing; the count does not exist. **Fix:** marking a job done collapses the row (height, opacity, a 12 px slide, 320 ms `--ease-in`), the count badge ticks down with a 1.25× bump, a toast offers Undo for 7 s, and the empty list says "Nothing needs you right now". Demo: click Review the names; `strip-range-and-done.png`. (What "done" writes back is the engine's business; the page needs an endpoint or stores it in `localStorage` until then.) *Medium.*
10. **Reduced motion (A and C: nothing; live page: a blunt `* { transition: none !important }`).** **Fix:** the tokens go to 0 ms under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (and a `data-motion="reduce"` attribute for testing): nothing slides, pops, lifts, counts or pulses; opacity-only fades stay at 120 ms (a crossfade is not motion and avoids the "did it change?" problem); the rebuilding LED becomes a static halo; scrolling jumps. The demo proves it: with the media query on, zero animations with a duration are running after load, and after a range change only 120 ms opacity fades. Demo: the Reduce motion checkbox; `demo-reduced-motion.png`. *Medium.*
11. **Touch (A and C).** A's tooltips are dead on a phone, C's titles too; a 6–8 px dot is not a target. **Fix:** 24 px hit circle, first tap pins the tooltip (hint text changes to "tap again to open the project"), second tap opens, tap elsewhere closes. Demo: `demo-phone-tap.png`, `demo-phone-tap2.png`. *Medium.*
12. **Tab switching (A instant and scroll-to-top; C dead links).** **Fix:** old view fades out 200 ms, new view fades up 6 px over 200 ms, the underline slides, each tab remembers its scroll position. Demo: `demo-projects-tab.png`. *Low-medium.*
13. **"+13 more · open Pipeline" (A) / "show all 23 as a list" (C).** A sends you to another tab to see five more cards; C swaps the table. **Fix:** reveal in place with a 40 ms stagger (done for "+14 more"). *Low.*
14. **C's integration bar stretches its text.** Not motion, but hover reveals it: `preserveAspectRatio="none"` on an SVG with `<text>` inside distorts the labels. Same fix as finding 4: size the SVG to its width. *Low.*
15. **A's `.job` and `.proj` tooltips repeat the card's own text** ("open Andromeda in Targets", the evidence line). A tooltip that repeats what is visible is noise; the one that adds a fact (the sessions in a night, the exact hours in a bar) earns its place. **Fix:** tooltips on marks only. *Low.*

## What A and C got right, and the blend should keep

- A's scope strips (one lane per scope, a dot per night) are exactly the right unit for motion: they are the thing that moves on a range change and the thing that gains a dot on a refresh. The demo keeps them, with A's empty-night dots at 30 nights only.
- C's lanes with the quiet band and the three indicators are the right expansion; they just need to arrive gently and at full width.
- C's night-sky header with the hero is the right stage for the freshness pill.
- The live page's tooltip (`hoverable()`: built on enter, follows focus, moved on pointermove) is the right skeleton; the demo keeps its content model and changes the timing and anchoring.

## What the demo could not test here

Headless Chromium only. Safari and Edge on Brett's machines still need a look at: the `r` transition on dots (Safari may jump the radius instead of easing it: harmless), `scrollBy({behavior:"smooth"})` while a height transition runs, and `transform-box: fill-box` on SVG groups (supported in all three, but the Mac Safari build should confirm). None of these affect the layout or the data.
